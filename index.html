Christ Becomes Present

Every day, in hundreds of thousands of churches around the world, bread and wine are consecrated and become the actual Body and Blood of Jesus Christ, while still looking, tasting, and feeling like bread and wine. This site exists to explain that belief: where it comes from, why the Church has always taught it, and what it means for you, whether you are a lifelong Catholic, a skeptic, or someone simply looking for Jesus.

The Living Bread

Throughout history, the Church has investigated extraordinary events in which the appearance of the Eucharist itself has changed — hosts that became visible flesh and blood, consecrated hosts that survived fires, floods, and decay. These are not the foundation of Catholic belief in the Real Presence — Christ's own words are — but they are striking confirmations of it.
